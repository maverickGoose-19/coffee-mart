# PRD v2: Marketplace Expansion

## Goal

Extend the MVP into a fuller sourcing network after the trust and logistics layer is proven.

## Potential Expansion Areas

- buyer accounts with saved searches and favorite suppliers
- broker and freight forwarder accounts
- quote requests for larger export orders
- document vault for export paperwork
- shipment milestone automation
- deal room for negotiation and contracting
- analytics for suppliers on inquiry-to-shipment conversion
- hybrid and learned recommendation models trained on inquiry, sample, and deal outcomes
- EUDR-aware recommendation scoring for EU buyers

## Graduation Signals

Consider moving from MVP to v2 when:

- a repeatable set of suppliers consistently ships samples to US buyers
- sample-to-deal conversion is measurable
- manual operations are becoming the primary bottleneck
