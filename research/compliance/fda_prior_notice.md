# FDA Prior Notice

For MVP planning, treat FDA Prior Notice as required for all US-bound food imports, including coffee samples.

Product implications:

- responsibility must be assigned before shipment
- filing status must be trackable on the inquiry
- the platform should not allow US sample shipment workflows to ignore this field

MVP data points:

- `prior_notice_required`
- `prior_notice_filed`
- `prior_notice_filed_by`
